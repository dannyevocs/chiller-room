    Technician training scenarios

    • A Begin Simulation button on the toolbar opens the training exercises. Nothing changes in the room until you start one, and ending the simulation puts the plant back to normal. All readings, alarms and faults are simulated training data.
    • Routine Inspection: walk the round, record each inspection point as normal or abnormal (identifying the supply and return lines on the way), and find one randomly chosen abnormality: a closed standby pump valve, high leaving water, a pump seal leak, a raised refrigerant reading or warm condenser water.
    • Valve Identification: find five valves from their function, with the names hidden until you answer. Wrong picks explain what that valve does, and a hint appears.
    • Pump Failure: "PUMP P-01 (CHWP-1): FAULT DETECTED". Acknowledge it, locate the pump, read its drive and both pumps, then choose what the evidence shows and how to respond, including unsafe options and their consequences.
    • Water Leak: a drip and puddle at one of three real connections (a pump seal on each circuit, or a chiller valve flange behind the rail). Find it, name the equipment, choose the accurate report and the safe response.
    • High Chilled Water Temperature: the chiller panel, gauges and trend show warm condenser water limiting the chiller. Compare supply and return, find the likely cause and respond.
    • Equipment Knowledge Mode adds common issues and what technicians inspect to every equipment panel. Each exercise ends with a score by category, the objectives met and feedback, with Retry.
    • Responses follow general good practice and need review against the facility's own procedures before use in real training.
